# Reference photos

Photos taken by Vikas T G around Bengaluru, used as reference for textures,
signage, street clutter and colour grading. Only add photos you took yourself
or that carry an open licence (record the source and licence in `CREDITS.md`).
Do not add screenshots of Google Maps or Street View.

Useful subjects: shopfront rows, PG and To-Let boards, compound walls, autos
at a stand, bus stops, footpaths and potholes, overhead cables, tree canopy
over a road, the same street at 9 AM, 5 PM and after rain.
